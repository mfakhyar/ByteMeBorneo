## Project Summary
ASLI is a mobile application designed to preserve and share indigenous stories, traditions, and knowledge across ASEAN countries.

The platform allows users to explore tribes through an interactive map, listen to folktales, and contribute their own cultural stories using AI speech-to-text technology.

Our goal is to promote cultural preservation, education, and appreciation of indigenous heritage.
